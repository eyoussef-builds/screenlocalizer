Introducing Orion 2: our most capable model yet

June 10, 2026 · 6 min read

Today we're releasing Orion 2, a new flagship model that sets the state of the art across reasoning, coding, and multilingual benchmarks.

Orion 2 features a 1M-token context window and scores 94.2% on GPQA Diamond, up from 88.1% in the previous generation. Long-horizon agentic tasks see the largest gains: the model can now sustain multi-hour coding sessions with 3× fewer intervention requests, and tool-calling reliability improves to 99.1% on our internal harness.

The model is available starting today in the API, with pricing unchanged from the previous generation. Developers can opt into the new fast mode for latency-sensitive applications.

We're also publishing a detailed system card describing our safety evaluations, red-teaming process, and deployment safeguards.
